## Brand & Style

The visual identity embodies the intimacy of heritage home cooking re-imagined as an elevated, Michelin-caliber sensory experience. It targets discerning diners who value authentic culinary craft, emotional warmth, and atmospheric dining. The digital environment must evoke the hushed, ambient glow of an exclusive evening dinner service—warm light refracting through fine glassware, dark stone countertops, and the aromatic vibrancy of rich spices.

The aesthetic fuses **Refined Culinary Luxury** with **Subtle Atmospheric Glassmorphism**. Dark, velvety surfaces provide an unobtrusive stage that lets food imagery command attention, while frosted translucent panels and faint warm-amber glows add modern tactility and depth. The tension between the classic editorial grace of a high-contrast serif and the crisp, approachable structure of contemporary geometric sans typography preserves an inviting, heartfelt tone without sacrificing bespoke sophistication.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in low-light fine-dining atmospheres, pairing a deep, charcoal-black baseline with the radiant heat of hearth embers and spun gold.

- **Primary (`#E4572E` - Burnt Terracotta / Hearth Orange):** Used for focal action points, reservation calls-to-action, active indicators, and expressive badge highlights. It conveys culinary heat and zest.
- **Secondary (`#F4B860` - Warm Gold / Gilded Honey):** Applied to ratings, chef notes, subtle border highlights, and delicate decorative accents that demand quiet reverence.
- **Backgrounds (`#0B0B0B` Canvas, `#141414` Surface Level 1):** Dense, near-black tones engineered to preserve deep visual contrast and eliminate eye strain in ambient dining light.
- **Text Hierarchy:** High-contrast headings and essential labels use `#F5F1EA` (Warm Cream) to avoid the clinical harshness of pure white. Secondary editorial metadata, menu ingredients, and captions utilize `#A8A29A` (Muted Linen).
- **Glass Tinting:** Glass containers use `#141414` with opacities between 55% and 75%, paired with hair-thin borders tinted with `#F5F1EA` at 8% to 12% opacity to catch light like the rim of a crystal tumbler.

## Typography

Typography orchestrates an editorial balance between dramatic, artisanal flourish and crystal-clear utility:

- **Headings (Playfair Display):** Conveys the prestige of classical fine dining and printed tasting menus. Titles employ tighter tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) to create dense, impactful layouts. Italic variants are reserved for dish origins, wine pairings, and poetic chef descriptors.
- **Body & Functional UI (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Grounded, approachable, and highly legible against dark backgrounds. Generous line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) ensure ingredient breakdowns, allergy advisories, and narrative courses read effortlessly on all displays.
- **Micro-Copy & Pricing:** Prices are set in Plus Jakarta Sans SemiBold to keep numbers immediately scannable, while labels and tags utilize uppercase styling with generous tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to mimic boutique packaging typography.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is governed by an adaptive 12-column grid designed to provide luxurious breathing room around visual culinary assets.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, `margin-lg` (3rem), and `gutter` (1.5rem). Cards, booking steps, and tasting menus snap to 4, 6, or 8-column modules, allowing large editorial dish photography to sit alongside frosted informational surfaces.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `margin` (1.5rem), and `gutter-sm` (1rem). Multi-column dish listings collapse gracefully into alternating split cards or fluid two-column layouts.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin-sm` (1rem), and `gutter-sm` (1rem). Content stacks vertically into single-column glass cards. Large imagery uses 4:3 or 1:1 aspect ratios, with critical details overlaid on translucent glass panels.
- **Rhythm:** Internal component spacing adheres to an 8px base rhythm (`space-xs` through `space-xl`). Menu item details rely on `space-sm` between title and description, and `space-lg` to separate distinct course tiers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined glassmorphic system that mirrors candlelight passing through translucent, smoked crystal surfaces.

- **Base Layer (Elevation 0):** Canvas `#0B0B0B` with an ultra-subtle warm radial gradient (`rgba(228, 87, 46, 0.04)` centered in hero sections) simulating localized hearth warmth.
- **Translucent Glass Cards (Elevation 1):** Background `rgba(20, 20, 20, 0.65)` layered with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(140%)`. Outlined with a top-weighted 1px border: `linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(245, 241, 234, 0.14) 0%, rgba(245, 241, 234, 0.03) 100%)`. Soft, downward shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Floating Modals & Reservation Drawers (Elevation 2):** Background `rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`. Border is accented with subtle warm amber: `rgba(244, 184, 96, 0.2)`. Cast shadow: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 32px 0 rgba(228, 87, 46, 0.08)`.
- **Interactive Focus & Hover:** Elements on hover do not jump abruptly in elevation; instead, their glass border increases to `rgba(244, 184, 96, 0.35)` and an ambient, warm diffusion halo (`0 0 20px rgba(228, 87, 46, 0.15)`) emerges behind the container.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Roundedness Level 2** (base radius of 0.5rem / 8px). This geometry strikes a delicate equilibrium: soft enough to feel warm and organic like culinary prep, but disciplined enough to maintain the crisp architecture of modern high-end web design.

- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Selectors):** `0.5rem` (8px) radius.
- **Standard Cards, Menu Items, and Modals:** `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) radius, preventing corners from feeling sharp against frosted backgrounds.
- **Hero Banners & Feature Highlights:** `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) for expansive visual showcases.
- **Pills & Badges:** Distinctly circular or full-pill (`9999px`) reserved purely for status tags, chef's specials, dietary indicators, and small iconography.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Reservations, Order Placement):** Solid `#E4572E` fill with `#F5F1EA` text. 0.5rem border radius. Subtle inner shadow (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`) and an ambient glow on hover (`box-shadow: 0 4px 20px rgba(228, 87, 46, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary Action (View Menu, Dietary Filters):** Translucent glass fill (`rgba(245, 241, 234, 0.05)`), 1px border (`rgba(245, 241, 234, 0.15)`), and text in `#F5F1EA`. On hover, fills with `rgba(244, 184, 96, 0.1)` and border transitions to `#F4B860`.
- **Tertiary / Text Link:** Uppercase `label-md` in `#F4B860`, tracked `0.06em`, accompanied by an animated hairline underline that expands on hover.

### Glass Cards & Menu Tiles
- Contained inside `rounded-lg` (16px) glass panels with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`.
- Food photography sits cleanly within the card using an inner `rounded-md` frame with a subtle vignette edge to prevent hard breaks against the dark surface.
- Price tags are anchored at the top right in `#F4B860` with semi-bold Plus Jakarta Sans.

### Chips & Dietary Badges
- Compact pill-shaped containers (`height: 28px`, padding `0 12px`).
- Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` with a delicate 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- Typography: `label-tag` (11px, bold) in `#A8A29A`. Active or selected chips transition to `#E4572E` background tint and white text.

### Form Inputs & Date/Time Selectors
- Background: Dark glass inset `rgba(11, 11, 11, 0.6)`.
- Border: 1px solid `rgba(245, 241, 234, 0.12)`.
- Text: `#F5F1EA` for entered values; `#A8A29A` with 50% opacity for placeholders.
- Focus State: Smooth border transition to `#F4B860` with an amber focal ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(244, 184, 96, 0.15)`).

### Tasting Lists & Course Chronologies
- Clean, uncluttered vertical rows bounded by horizontal divider lines tinted at `rgba(245, 241, 234, 0.06)`.
- Playfair Display titles sit left-aligned, paired with dashed hairline guide leaders running horizontally toward right-aligned pricing and pairing badges.

### Specialty: "Chef's Signature" Callout
- Elevated card framed in a dual-stop hairline gradient border (`#F4B860` fading to transparent).
- Watermarked background with low-opacity script lettering or heritage culinary emblems, crowned with an amber badge for featured heritage specialties.
