Our Heritage & Craft

Our Story

Tucked away along Chowdhurypara Road in Barasat, Jayeeta's Kitchen was born from a profound love for the comforting simplicity of home cooking. We set out with a singular purpose: to craft unhurried, nourishing delicacies prepared with the same heartfelt warmth and honesty you would find at your family dining table.

From the gentle hiss of our signature butter-drizzled dosas crisping on cast iron, to piping-hot steamed idlis, tangy chatpata chaats, fluffy protein-packed omelettes, and hand-churned cold lassis—every single recipe is prepared fresh daily with uncompromised quality. We never cut corners, because to us, food is an offering of care.
